# Open architecture and implementation ownership

Status: recorded product direction; legal terms unresolved. This document is not a license and does not modify LICENSE.

- Forge is an open specification for independent actor/service interoperability.
- Roots is an open specification for portable, searchable, developable knowledge, graph and tensor semantics, and exchange. Independent implementations and integrations are welcome.
- The WDS Roots application is the owner's product; other developers can make and own their own compatible applications. The owner's intended direction permits use of design or source as a basis for independent implementations; exact permissions and boundaries must be formalized.
- ChristEye is a distinct WDS approach to developing understanding and may be used by the WDS implementation. Neither ChristEye nor the WDS SDK is a required dependency for Roots conformance.
- Knowledge owners retain their rights and permissions; interoperability never grants access or redistribution rights.

The existing root LICENSE expressly restricts use, adaptation and redistribution of source, designs and specifications. It conflicts with the intended open architecture and source reuse until appropriate legal terms are adopted. Public readability alone is not permission. Do not advertise released open-source or openly licensed specifications until resolved. Separate normative specification files, optional reference code, application-specific branding/assets, and third-party materials; choose explicit licenses for each, including any applicable patent terms and contribution policy. No license choice is implied by this draft. Keep existing notices intact pending owner decision.

Proposed conformance profiles: file-only reader/writer; query runtime; contributing/merging node; connected Forge participant. Publish schemas, canonical examples and language-independent tests; permit independently developed implementations without mandatory WDS registration or proprietary components. Authorization for a particular service or Tree is independent from protocol conformance.
