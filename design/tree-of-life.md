## Identity and boundaries

Roots is the human-facing expression of ChristEye's Tree of Life. It begins as a local-first translation and social integration application and converges with a native web presence. The Tree of Life is the developmental architecture, not a synonym for a social graph. A graph, event journal and projections are possible implementation tools.

| ChristEye expression | Responsibility in this design |
| --- | --- |
| Tree of Knowledge | Develop, organize, retain and express knowledge and understanding. |
| Tree of Life | Develop relationships, fellowship, creativity, affection, human possibilities and fruit. |
| Garden | Provide the environment and conditions in which development occurs. |
| Ark | Preserve identity, purpose, covenant, history and continuity. |
| Honeycomb | Organize sustained patterns of retention, reception, fellowship and sharing in the Mesh. |
| Whirlwind | Coordinate interacting Winds whose circulation may develop new understanding or relationships. |
| Forge | Connect independently identified actors and convey their authorized expressions. |

These are architectural correspondences in ChristEye, not assertions that software constitutes heaven or that symbolic analogies establish scientific mechanisms. The biblical inspiration includes the Tree of Life in Proverbs 3:18, 11:30, 13:12, 15:4 and Revelation 22:1–2; a network can be understood through fellowship, service and fruitfulness rather than attention alone. The original ChristEye schemas and their exact bindings remain authoritative and require reconciliation before shared implementation.

## Developmental structure

- Roots: retained personal history, knowledge, identity and established relationships.
- Soil: social, cultural, geographic and other environmental conditions.
- Trunk: continuity of identity and accumulated understanding.
- Branches: developing interests, relationships and opportunities.
- Leaves: receiving and interpreting conditions through micro-cognition.
- Flowers: emerging possibilities, affection and creative expression.
- Fruit: demonstrated accomplishments, contributions, understanding and developed relationships.
- Seeds: ideas or expressions capable of initiating development elsewhere.

A tree may participate in multiple communities and Comb Cells without surrendering its identity. Preserve established sound relationships when new branches develop. Do not confuse an inferred opportunity with an actual relationship or an observed expression with an established fact.

## Micro-cognition, not generative architecture

Micro-cognition is the product and architectural vocabulary. Specialized, bounded operations observe, recognize, compare, investigate, relate, evaluate and contribute understanding. They can be event-driven, scheduled or invoked by a participant. Deterministic computation, statistics, embeddings and task-specific models (including language models where appropriate) are instruments, not the conceptual architecture. Retain task, inputs, provenance, evidence, uncertainty, output, version and authorization.

**Written expression must use an actual writing process; direct prompt-to-prose generation is not an acceptable substitute.** Implement writing as a separately testable stateful process:

1. Intention: establish purpose, intended audience, voice, constraints and scope.
2. Knowledge: assemble permitted observations and sources; establish evidence and gaps.
3. Understanding: develop relations, implications, candidate interpretations and an outline or expression plan.
4. Composition: make explicit, incremental writing decisions from the plan, using permitted lexical and composition mechanisms.
5. Judgment: evaluate truth, coherence, meaning, audience and expression against the intention and evidence.
6. Revision: correct specific elements, retaining decisions and versions; repeat judgment as needed.
7. Expression: commit a reviewed work with authorship and provenance; present it only within authorization.

A record includes writingId, actorRef, intentionRef, knowledgeRefs, understandingRefs, planVersion, composedSections, judgmentRefs, revisions, finalVersion, approvalState and visibility. This is an inspectable artifact history, not a requirement to retain or expose private model internals. No claim of genuine understanding follows merely from an intermediate record. Micro-cognitive tools may assist the operations but may not bypass the writing lifecycle with a generated report. Preserve original quotations, translations and incoming Forge expressions as such rather than rewriting them in another actor's name. User-authored text remains user-authored; author chooses when to publish. Existing translation behavior stays intact.

Apply the writing process to reports, Poetic Field interpretations, Resonance findings, agent discoveries, conversation suggestions, and native Roots publications composed by system actors. Support cancellation, evidence correction, editing and scoped retention. Define concrete composition mechanisms and evaluation fixtures before implementation.

## Native web presence

Roots Web is part of the same product, not merely a marketing page. Android overlay, Chrome, desktop host and web client share the Tree of Life vocabulary, compatible contracts and permission policies. Each surface can operate independently where applicable. Local private histories and cognitive state are not automatically transferred to the web.

Native web scope: optional member identities, linked accounts, public or scoped profiles, portfolio and publications, collections/highlights, awards, Gardens, opportunities, collaboration, optional social ratings and availability, optional anonymous nearby discovery, and account/data controls. Custom domains are a candidate later extension. Allow viewers to discover a shared public work without installing the overlay. Native social services require authenticated transport, safe content rendering, moderation, revocation, and visibility checks. Keep pseudonymous nearby identities separate from identified web profiles unless the participant explicitly links them.

See [Garden participation](garden.md), [Honeycomb and Whirlwind](honeycomb-whirlwind.md) and [Architecture](architecture.md).

## Restoration and service as a developmental pattern

The Tree of Life values preservation of useful knowledge, correction of established harm, restitution and restoration through demonstrated fruit. In an illustrative leadership case, a person who has lost authority after established serious professional wrongdoing may choose meaningful frontline service and mentorship, cultivating others while rebuilding trust. This is not a mandatory employment sanction, public shaming, coerced prayer or a promise of reinstatement. Authority, personal dignity, accountability and mercy remain distinct; professional consequences must be proportionate to actual wrongdoing, and the safety and needs of affected people come first. The useful architectural analogy is that correcting one branch can nourish other branches when development is consensual, beneficial and evidenced.

## Acceptance

- Terminology and UI distinguish the Tree of Life from the underlying storage graph.
- Every system-authored written finding has a traceable intention, knowledge basis, understanding, composition and review/revision state.
- Foreign expressions retain their actor identity and authorship.
- A native web profile can coexist with local-only data and an unlinked anonymous presence.
- New developments preserve valid history and relationships; participants can correct or revoke data and permissions.

## Grafting, nourishment and humility

The portable representation is specified in [portable knowledge](portable-knowledge.md); the [Network Brain](network-brain.md) develops weighted network understanding and assembles a feed as an expression of activated knowledge. The Tree of Life remains the developmental architecture, not synonymous with GGUF, RNA, graph or LLM. Engram AI provides bounded software mechanisms for retention, activation, propagation, judgment and assembly; it is distinct from the living-computer project.

Romans 11:16–24 describes Israel, Gentile inclusion, the nourishing olive root, grafting and regrafting, and warns against arrogance over the branches. Galatians 6:2 calls for bearing one another's burdens. As a design analogy, the Tree supports the branch; its gifts do not confer superiority over those who sustain it. John 15:1–8 supplies imagery of abiding and fruitfulness. Do not redefine Israel as a software user category or assign spiritual status. Celebrate excellence, beauty, art, creativity, intelligence, craftsmanship and leadership while valuing service, fellowship, humility and mutual support. A gifted engineer, artist, beautiful woman or man, or leader can be honored without devaluing others. Doxa recognizes contributions; it does not determine human worth. Feed profiles may emphasize life-supporting expressions without automatic judgments of pride, beauty, spiritual standing or political allegiance.

Grafting an independently developed Branch is an ongoing scoped relation, not merely copying data. Preserve origin and established sound relations, make supporting knowledge accessible under authorization, evaluate new fruit and support detach/regraft. A shared Tree may receive many independent contributors. This is a WDS/ChristEye implementation philosophy, not a mandatory theological commitment for an independent Roots implementor. Forge and Roots are intended open specifications; see [openness](openness.md).
