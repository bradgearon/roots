# Roots design area

Status: exploratory product design, 2026-11-25. Copyright (c) 2026 We Develop Software LLC. All rights reserved. See [proprietary license](../LICENSE).

Roots is both an intended open specification for portable, searchable, developable knowledge and a WDS-owned local-first social integration application that converges with a native web presence. Forge is likewise an open specification; independent implementations need not use ChristEye, Engram AI or a proprietary SDK. Existing legal terms remain unchanged pending an explicit license decision. It is the human-facing expression of ChristEye's Tree of Life, not merely a social graph. A feed tells you what is happening; Roots helps you understand what is growing. Honeycomb organizes sustainable patterns and Whirlwind coordinates interacting Winds whose circulation may produce new development.

## Principles

- Augment existing networks rather than require people to abandon them.
- Preserve source identity, context, attribution, history and user agency.
- Translation, content intelligence, analytics, agents, ratings and dating are independent modules.
- Feature enablement, participation, availability and visibility are independent controls.
- Distinguish measured activity, subjective ratings and micro-cognitive interpretations; retain evidence and uncertainty.
- Use an actual writing process for system-authored expression, never direct prompt-to-prose generation as a substitute.
- Preserve established sound relations while evaluating and authorizing Honeycomb reweaving. Judge Whirlwind by demonstrated outcomes, not activity alone.
- Keep private information local by default. Remote inference, sync and sharing require scoped authorization.
- [Transport security](transport-security.md): retain normal TLS and platform connection security; never add a second application-level encryption layer to knowledge, messages or archives.
- Preserve existing translation and Forge capabilities; new functionality is additive.

Working names (Doxa, Gardens, Resonance, Poetic Field, Kairos and Tendrils) are proposals. Maintain decisions, assumptions, open questions and acceptance criteria here; mark behavior implemented only after code and verification exist.
